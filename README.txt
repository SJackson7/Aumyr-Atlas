AUMYR POCKET ATLAS | ALTERNATE GRACE
=================================

An independent, phone-friendly live clock + calendar + simulated-weather app.

WHAT YOU GET
- Real-time Aumyr clock with seven-day week, twelve 28-day moons, and four 84-day seasons.
- Your device's latitude and longitude projected (for fun) onto the Aumyr map.
- Adjustable fictional coordinates, map-tap selection, and optional manual Earth coordinates.
- Fixed two-hour simulated weather forecasts, temperature, wind, rain chance and humidity.
- Phone-friendly home-screen icon and service worker for offline functionality.

HOW TO RUN ON AN IPHONE WITH GPS
1. Unzip and put the CONTENTS of this folder on any HTTPS static web host.
   For instance, create a new site with a static-hosting service. The website entrypoint is index.html.
2. Open that HTTPS link in Safari.
3. Tap 'Find my Earth location', then allow Safari to access your location.
4. To keep it as a phone app, tap Share > Add to Home Screen.

IMPORTANT
- iPhone Files previews and file:// pages may disallow geolocation, service workers, or JavaScript. Use an HTTPS address for reliable GPS.
- GPS coordinates remain on your device and are not sent to the map website or an API.
- The HD-2D overview is bundled locally and works offline. The coordinate grid remains usable if the browser fails to load the image.
- The weather is a repeatable, lightweight fictional simulation, not the exact full GM map's weather engine.
- The Aumyr prime meridian is tied to UTC solely to make a live synchronized entertainment clock. That linkage is not a new canonical lore rule.
- The projection maps all Earth longitudes (-180 to 180) into Aumyr (-45 to 45), and all Earth latitudes (-90 to 90) into Aumyr (20 to 65 N).
